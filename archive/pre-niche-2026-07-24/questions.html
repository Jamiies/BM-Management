About BM Management

What is BM Management?

BM Management is the practice through which Bill Mellenthin, CPA provides financial leadership and accounting services. The name is short for Bill Mellenthin Management. It is a single-member Colorado limited liability company, owned entirely by Bill — there are no partners, outside investors, or silent owners.

In practice that means the person you talk to is the person doing the work. Engagements are not staffed out to junior associates, and nothing is passed along to someone you have never met.

Who would I be contracting with?

BM Management, LLC — the Colorado limited liability company — not Bill personally. Engagement letters, invoices, and payments are all in the company's name. If your accounts payable process needs a W-9, insurance certificate, or vendor form completed, just ask and it will be sent over.

What does a CPA actually do for a business like mine?

It depends on what you need. Some owners want reliable financial statements and a clean set of books. Others need someone to interpret those numbers — where the margin is going, whether the cash will be there in ninety days, what a lender will think. Bill does both, and the conversation usually starts by figuring out which one you're actually asking for.

What kind of experience does Bill bring?

Well over twenty years. He began as a CPA in a public accounting firm, then served as Chief Financial Officer in aerospace manufacturing and in cannabusinesses, and as Director of Finance in hospitality and food & beverage. Much of that work was in companies going through acquisitions, reorganization, refinancing, or fast growth — environments where the finance function has to change alongside the business.

Working Together

Do I need a full-time CFO, or something smaller?

Most growing businesses need CFO-level thinking long before they need a full-time CFO salary. A fractional arrangement gives you the judgment when it matters — monthly reporting, planning, lender conversations — without carrying the position year-round. If the business genuinely needs someone full time, Bill will tell you that too.

How do engagements usually work?

Three common shapes:

  • Ongoing — a recurring monthly commitment.
  • Project-based — a defined scope with a defined end.
  • Interim — filling a CFO or controller seat during a search.

Scope & Logistics

Do you prepare tax returns?

[ Bill to confirm — this is an important one to get right. Suggested if he does not: “Bill's focus is financial leadership, reporting, and accounting operations rather than tax preparation. He works alongside your existing tax CPA, and can recommend one if you need it.” ]

Will you work with my existing bookkeeper or accounting staff?

Yes — that's often the best arrangement. Bill frequently works above an existing bookkeeper or accounting team, providing the review, structure, and senior judgment while the day-to-day stays in place. Part of the job is leaving that team more capable than he found it.

My books are a mess. Is that a problem?

No. It's one of the most common reasons someone calls. Cleaning up records, reconciling accounts that haven't been touched in a while, and getting to a trustworthy starting balance is ordinary work — and it's the necessary first step before any reporting means anything.
