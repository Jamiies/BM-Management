Twenty-plus years of hands-on experience

Clarity in complicated situations

BM Management is the practice of Bill Mellenthin, CPA. He has worked as a CPA in public accounting, as a Chief Financial Officer in aerospace manufacturing and cannabusinesses, and as a Director of Finance in hospitality and food & beverage. That range matters in this work — forensic engagements, receiverships, and estates all depend on reading an unfamiliar set of books accurately, and quickly.

Because the practice is deliberately small, the person you meet is the person who does the work. Engagements are scoped with a defined end, and a good one leaves the department, the estate, or the entity running properly afterward.
