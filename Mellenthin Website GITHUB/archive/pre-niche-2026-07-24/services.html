Services

Senior financial expertise, scoped to what your business actually needs — from a focused project to ongoing leadership.

Draft placeholder. These eight services were drafted from Bill's background as a starting point. He should cut the ones he doesn't want, add anything missing, and reword the descriptions in his own voice.

Every business is at a different stage. Some need a steady hand month to month; others need someone to step in for a specific stretch — a transaction, a system change, a gap in the finance team. Bill works both ways.

01 Fractional CFO

Executive-level financial leadership without a full-time hire. Bill sits alongside ownership on strategy, capital, and performance — then makes sure the numbers behind those decisions hold up.

  • Financial strategy and planning
  • Lender, investor, and board reporting
  • Capital structure and refinancing support
  • KPI and performance reporting

02 Controller Services

Day-to-day ownership of the accounting function — the close, the reconciliations, the controls — so the financial statements are ready when you need them.

  • Monthly and year-end close
  • General ledger and reconciliations
  • Accounting policies and procedures
  • Oversight of accounting staff

03 Financial Reporting

Accurate, timely statements prepared with a public accounting discipline, plus management reporting that tells owners what the numbers actually mean.

  • GAAP financial statements
  • Management and departmental reporting
  • Consolidations and multi-entity reporting
  • Covenant and compliance reporting

04 Budgeting & Forecasting

Budgets and models built from how the business actually runs — useful for planning, not just for the file.

  • Annual budgets and rolling forecasts
  • Cash-flow forecasting
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Budget-to-actual variance review

05 Audit & Review Preparation

Get ready for the auditors before they arrive. Bill has been on both sides of the engagement and knows what will be asked for.

  • Schedule and workpaper preparation
  • Account analysis and clean-up
  • Serving as the point of contact for auditors
  • Resolving prior-year findings

06 Transaction Support

Support through acquisitions, reorganizations, and refinancing — the environments Bill has spent much of his career leading companies through.

  • Buy-side and sell-side due diligence support
  • Quality-of-earnings preparation assistance
  • Post-acquisition integration of accounting
  • Lender and refinancing packages

07 Process & Systems

When the business has outgrown its accounting processes, Bill rebuilds them — documented, repeatable, and staffed by people who understand them.

  • Accounting system selection and implementation
  • Internal controls and segregation of duties
  • Close-process design and documentation
  • Staff training and mentoring

08 Closely Held Business Advisory

A trusted sounding board for owners — someone who understands both the business and what matters to the family or partners behind it.

  • Owner and partner reporting
  • Profitability and pricing analysis
  • Coordination with your tax CPA and attorney
  • Succession and transition planning support

Working Together

Ways to Engage

Three common arrangements — the right one depends on the size of the business and what's in front of it.

1

Ongoing

A recurring monthly commitment — the close, the reporting, and a standing conversation about what the numbers are showing.

2

Project-Based

A defined scope with a defined end: an audit to prepare for, a system to implement, a set of books to clean up, a transaction to get through.

3

Interim

Stepping into a CFO or controller seat while a search is underway, keeping the finance function steady through the gap.

Not sure which one fits?

That's a normal place to start. A short conversation is usually enough to figure out what a business actually needs — and whether Bill is the right person for it.
